## 🌐 Overview & Trade Secretariat Backing

**[Coffee Supplier Indonesia](https://www.coffeesupplier.biz.id/)** operates a direct trade export network connecting **143 smallholder farmer cooperatives** across 6 Indonesian volcanic microclimates directly to global commercial roasteries and green coffee importers. 

Operating under the oversight of the **Indonesian Coffee Association (ASKI) Central Secretariat** in Jakarta, we ensure complete supply chain transparency, direct farmgate pricing, and 100% regulatory compliance for European, American, Middle Eastern, and Asian destination ports.

## 🛡️ European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) Compliance

We provide 100% verified compliance for Regulation (EU) 2023/1115. Every FCL container shipping to European destination ports (*Hamburg, Bremen, Rotterdam, Genoa, Antwerp, Barcelona*) includes:

- 📍 **GPS Polygon Farm Geolocation**: Exact boundary coordinates for all smallholder plots.
- 📜 **Automated Due Diligence Statement (DDS)**: XML compliance file synced with EU Traces system.
- 🌲 **Forest Canopy Cutoff Audit**: Zero deforestation post-December 31, 2020.
- 🔗 **Learn More**: [EUDR Traceability Guide](https://www.coffeesupplier.biz.id/eudr-compliance/)

## 🚢 Ocean Freight & Logistics Specifications

- **Container Loading Standard**: 20ft FCL Dry Container (320 Jute Bags @ 60KG Net Each = 19,200 KG / 19.2 Metric Tons).

- **Port of Loading**: Belawan (IDBLW - Medan), Tanjung Priok (IDTPP - Jakarta), Tanjung Perak (IDSUB - Surabaya).
